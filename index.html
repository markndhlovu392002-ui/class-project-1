The problem

Academic resources are everywhere — except where students need them.

Students may have to search through WhatsApp groups, emails, shared drives, classmates, and different websites to find material for a course.

💬 WhatsAppScattered class resources
☁️ Shared DrivesHard to organize
📧 EmailOld messages get lost
🌐 WebsitesNot always course-specific

Students need a trusted starting point.

The proposed solution brings course resources together and makes it easier to identify materials that have been provided or recommended by lecturers.


See Our Solution →
Our solution

One place for course resources.

StudyHub is designed around courses, making relevant resources easier to discover, organize, and understand.

🎓

Course-Centered

Resources are organized around courses so students can focus on what they are studying.

✓

Lecturer Validation

Resources can carry a clear lecturer-recommended or lecturer-provided label.

🗂️

Organized Materials

Books, slides, past papers, tutorials, and other resources can be grouped by type.

Features

📚

Verified Resources
